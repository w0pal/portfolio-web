# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary audience is the owner of the site and evaluators of their work (recruiters, collaborators, peers) who need a credible, clear view of projects and growth over time. The owner uses the site as an evolving personal archive for work, writing, and technical progression.

## Product Purpose
This portfolio exists to document work and learning in a way that compounds over time, while presenting a trustworthy professional signal to people evaluating capability. Success means a consistent timeline of meaningful project and blog entries, plus stronger professional credibility from visitors who can quickly understand strengths, craft, and trajectory.

## Brand Personality
Calm, technical, human. The voice should feel restrained and clear rather than loud, balancing precision with personal warmth. The emotional goal is confidence through clarity: visitors should feel they are seeing genuine work and thoughtful progression.

## Anti-references
Avoid ultra-corporate aesthetics and messaging. Do not drift into polished enterprise marketing language, impersonal stock-product framing, or visually cold, generic corporate templates that erase personal identity.

## Design Principles
1. Credibility through evidence: prioritize real artifacts (projects, writing, changelog-like progress) over claims.
2. Clarity over spectacle: keep hierarchy and copy straightforward so visitors understand value quickly.
3. Personal, not performative: express the maker's character without becoming noisy or gimmicky.
4. Consistent progression: make growth over time legible across portfolio and blog surfaces.
5. Restraint with intent: every visual decision should support trust, readability, and signal quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA for public-facing surfaces, including readable contrast, keyboard accessibility, and robust focus states. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid motion that blocks comprehension. Keep interactions and copy clear for a broad audience with varied visual and cognitive needs.
